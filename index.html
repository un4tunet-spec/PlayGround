    How to play

    1. Read the Teardown Manual (left). Only the current step can be removed.
    2. Select the tool shown for that step (1 2 3).
    3. Click each glowing bolt on the 3D engine to loosen it.
    4. When the part pulses green, click it to lift it to the workbench.
    5. Wrong tool or wrong part = damage + lost points. H shows a hint (−100).
    6. Strip all 25 assemblies to expose the core shafts and win.
